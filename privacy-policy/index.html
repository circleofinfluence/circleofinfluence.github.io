2. Types of Data We Collect

Personal Data

While using the Service, We may ask You to provide certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You, including:

  • Email address
  • First name and last name

Payment Data

To subscribe to paid tiers of the Service, You will need to provide payment information (such as credit card or bank account details). Payment processing is handled by our third-party payment processor, Stripe, and We do not directly store full payment card numbers on Our own systems. We retain limited billing records — such as invoice history, transaction amounts, and dates — for accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes.

Instagram and Other Connected Account Data

The Service allows You to connect Your Instagram professional account(s) in order to schedule and publish content. When You connect an Instagram account, We collect and store:

  • an access token issued by Instagram, which authorizes the Service to publish content on Your behalf;
  • Your Instagram user ID;
  • Your Instagram username and account type;
  • the identifier of each post the Service publishes to Your account, and the date and time it was published.

We never receive or store Your Instagram password. Access is granted by You through Instagram's own login and consent screen. The Service requests only the instagram_business_basic and instagram_business_content_publish permissions when You connect an account, and does not use any other Instagram permission.

Accordingly, We do not access or store Your existing posts, Your comments or the comments of others, Your direct messages, Your follower or audience information, or Your engagement, insights, or analytics metrics.

This data is accessed via Instagram's API and is subject to Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies in addition to this Privacy Policy.

You may disconnect a connected Instagram account at any time. See Section 6 for all available routes.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service. This may include Your Device's IP address, browser type and version, the pages of the Service You visit, the time and date of Your visit, time spent on those pages, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic data.

When You access the Service through a mobile device, We may also collect the type of mobile device You use, Your mobile device's unique ID, mobile operating system, the type of mobile browser You use, and other diagnostic data.

4. Retention of Your Personal Data

The Company retains Your Personal Data only as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, or as required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Where possible, We apply shorter retention periods and reduce identifiability by deleting, aggregating, or anonymizing data. Unless otherwise stated, the periods below are maximums, and We may delete or anonymize data sooner:

  • User Accounts: retained for the duration of Your account relationship, plus up to 24 months after account closure.
  • Customer support data (tickets, correspondence, chat transcripts): up to 24 months from ticket closure.
  • Usage Data (application statistics, server logs, IP addresses): up to 24 months for security and improvement purposes.
  • Instagram access tokens: retained only while the Connected Account remains connected. Deleted immediately when You disconnect the account in the Service, and within 30 days of account deletion or a deletion request.
  • Other connected Instagram account data: retained only while the account remains connected, and deleted from Our systems within the timeframes described in our User Data Deletion Policy once disconnected or upon account deletion.
  • Billing records (invoice history, transaction amounts, and dates): retained for up to 7 years to comply with financial recordkeeping and tax obligations, as described in our User Data Deletion Policy.

We may retain data beyond these periods where required by law, necessary to establish or defend legal claims, at Your explicit request, or due to technical limitations such as encrypted backup schedules. When retention periods expire, We delete or anonymize Personal Data; residual copies may briefly remain in encrypted backups consistent with our backup rotation and are not restored except for security, disaster recovery, or legal compliance purposes.

6. Delete Your Personal Data

You have the right to delete, or request that We assist in deleting, the Personal Data We have collected about You, including data associated with Connected Instagram accounts.

To disconnect a Connected Instagram account and delete its access token, You may use any of the following routes. You do not need to use more than one.

  1. Revoke access in Instagram. In the Instagram app, go to Settings → Apps and Websites → Active, and remove Circle of Influence. This revokes the token from Meta's side as well as ours, and is available to every connected account.
  2. Disconnect in the Service, where the publishing console is available to You. Selecting Disconnect deletes the stored access token immediately and ends Our ability to publish to that account.
  3. Contact Us at hello@circleofinfluence.io. We action deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by reply.

To delete other Personal Data, including Your Account, You may contact Us at the address above, or use the deletion options available in the Service.

Full details of our deletion process and timelines are set out in our User Data Deletion Policy. Please note We may need to retain certain information where We have a legal obligation or lawful basis to do so.
